# Benchmark expansion: source and reference-standard qualification

**November 6, 2026. Executed source qualification and annotation assessment; no additional clinical images, CNN inference, training or performance estimate.**

[Contract frozen before assessment](BENCHMARK_EXPANSION_CONTRACT_20261106.md) · [Results](../experiments/benchmark-source-qualification-20261106/) · [Offline assessment code](../scripts/assess_benchmark_sources.py)

## Decision

No additional original-image experiment was admitted under this contract. **Galar remains conditionally suitable for further published-annotation-run development**, but the source-level grouping/domain records needed for the requested diversity and leakage assessment are unresolved. A small resource-defined extension must not be sold as patient-independent or cross-device validation. The current external alternatives do not supply a verified complete, comparable blood-run reference standard with usable source/group rights and access.

This is not a finding that all further examination-only development is scientifically invalid, or that a larger Galar acquisition is technically impossible. It is a **bounded acquisition and evidence decision**: screen the full candidate population, identify what is missing, and avoid acquiring a convenience subset without resolving why it would improve the benchmark. No new generalization evidence or better AI is claimed.

**53.55% of recorded positive runs are short;16.03% are singletons.** Across599 adjacent positive-run pairs within the six positive examinations,41 are separated by one explicit blood-negative position,41 by two,65 by3–5, and452 by6+. Thus147/599 pairs (24.54%) are within five negative positions. This measures **annotation topology**, not label error or proof that neighbouring runs are the same lesion. Do not bridge those gaps, relabel frames, merge runs or change frozen denominators without a separate clinical reference-standard study.

The target also requires care. Only **298 of17,344 blood-positive positions** simultaneously carry `active bleeding`; **17,046 blood-positive positions do not**. Those are separate annotation fields, not interchangeable targets. Examinations33 and37 have zero `blood` positives but have20 and15 `hematin` positives. This confirms that **zero-blood does not mean normal, no bleeding history or no clinically relevant pathology**. These relationships do not establish annotation mistakes. They identify where a clinician-approved target definition matters before external transfer or diagnostic claims.

Examinations31–38 use native export stride5;39–40 use1. Short-run strata count positive **published positions**; they are not matched event durations across systems, and device/site cannot be inferred from stride. No time-based sensitivity is computed. Complete explicit labels in these exported positions permit published annotation-run calculations; they do not establish lesion-instance identities or all device exposures.

## Exact remaining scientific blockers and next executable experiment

1. **Galar source qualification:** obtain a standalone metadata-only export for1–8 and31–40 (excluding reserved outcome payloads), a deidentified patient-repeat/group map or an explicit custodian statement that it is unavailable, and authenticated per-examination device/site fields. A patient group cannot be fabricated from a study ID. Check original fitting/source overlap and source duplicates before claiming generalization.
2. **Reference-standard qualification:** keep the Galar `blood` target for the frozen annotation-run benchmark. To claim preservation of clinically important events, independently adjudicate short positive stretches, neighbouring runs and blood/hematin/active-bleeding distinctions; record lesion/event IDs and reference intervals with explicit uncertain/negative coverage. Do not manufacture exhaustive negatives from Kvasir/VideoCAP keyframes or CAPTIV8 weak clips.
3. **Resource gate:** after metadata qualification, freeze either the full31–40 expansion with funded private retention, or a smaller source-stratified cohort under an explicit new budget/eligibility amendment **before any new CNN outcomes**. The14.07GB minimum is a transparent planning result, not a request sent to anyone. No custodian or clinician message was sent.

**Next experiment most likely to improve the AI:** after qualification, conduct one group-excluded **training-diversity** comparison—original fitting population versus a prespecified expanded set of independently grouped positive examinations, unchanged model family and matched training budget—on a separately frozen complete-source benchmark with these geometry controls and short-run outcomes. First run the unchanged original CNN on that new benchmark to measure the actual transfer gap. This isolates the value of source diversity rather than selecting a larger architecture on known failures. If independent groups cannot be recovered, the work can still be described as examination-separated development, with no patient-generalization claim.

The current stage contributes **reference-standard and feasibility evidence, not new generalization or model-performance evidence**. PR66's conditional local-score result, the frozen11–20 negative primary,16-examination inconsistent alignment and temporal harms remain unchanged. No model is promoted, and original authors'61–80 remain unopened.
