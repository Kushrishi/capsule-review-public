## Candidate universe and selection
Screen Galar31–40 and the official Kvasir-Capsule, CAPTIV8, CAD-CAP, Rhode Island GI VCE, SEE-AI, WCEbleedGen and Red Lesion datasets. Use official source/annotation/group/domain/licence evidence, not existing CNN outcomes. Unknown is not pass. Report every candidate and every Galar examination, including zero-blood cases and unresolved fields. Do not infer patients or sites from age/sex, filenames, device appearance or embedding similarity.

The Galar expansion sampling frame is the whole31–40 export: NEW examinations31,32,33,34,37,38,39,40. Carry completed35/36 as historical descriptive context without downloads/inference/reanalysis. No opportunistic substitution of an easier/favourable examination. Negative blood annotations do not make an examination clinically normal. Screen all cases for leakage against original fitting examinations1–8 and inspected model-development sources. If patient linkage is unavailable, mark patient overlap unresolved: an examination-only descriptive study remains possible, but independent-patient generalization is prohibited.

## Admission, claims and bounded acquisition
An annotation-run development experiment requires permitted research use/version; complete source-examination identity and chronology; explicit target labels throughout the eligible published stream (unknowns fail); fixed native run definition and ontology; source-byte integrity and leakage assessment; private durable source/prediction capacity; and frozen cohort/analysis. Verify device/site records before making a domain-diversity claim. Clinical event preservation additionally requires clinician-adjudicated unique-event boundaries and a complete examination reference standard. Those are different claim levels; do not impose clinical-lesion truth merely to compute published annotation-run recall, or treat annotation-run recall as that truth.

Source/group leakage that cannot be resolved must be disclosed and restrict claims. For this requested diversity-focused expansion, missing source-level device/site evidence and patient-repeat mapping trigger source qualification rather than purchasing a larger uncharacterized convenience sample. Patient information is never imputed. A dataset with only selected labelled frames, weak clip labels or anatomy labels fails the same blood-run endpoint even when its videos and licence are available.

Bound: at most20MiB of new catalog/header/metadata transfer per candidate during qualification; no clinical image archive as a metadata fallback. New original-source transfer is capped at8GiB and requires all applicable evidence, a complete prespecified additional cohort with at least two new positive examinations and one zero-blood examination, and pre-audit verified private retention. This is a feasibility floor, not a power calculation or assurance of generalization; fewer cases cannot answer the requested diversity question. If no cohort meets this bound without outcome-driven selection or a scientific/metadata gap, publish the executed qualification/reference-standard result and no new AI-performance claim. Never acquire full69GB Galar archive. Do not open11–20 or61–80 payloads.
