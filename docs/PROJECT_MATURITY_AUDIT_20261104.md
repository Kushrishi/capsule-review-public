# Capsule Review — scientific and product maturity audit

**Reviewed 2026-11-04 against public `main` commit `1f8afe407fe18e1c1a70fb8ae8e1cc524d602373`.** This is a read-only synthesis of existing public results and inspected code, not a new experiment, independent clinical review, or verification of the private files in the previous Work cloud computer. Original clinical source/patient data and the authors' Galar 61–80 outcomes were not accessed.

## Executive decision

**Continue the project as an auditable, local research viewer and evidence-preservation investigation. Do not promote its blood CNN or claim a diagnostic/clinical benefit.** The end-to-end software works and the frozen scientific result is real, including a negative prespecified 10% primary outcome. The unresolved research question is whether AI-guided frame selection can **reliably** preserve distinct annotated evidence intervals better than simple uniform selection at a fixed workload across sufficiently independent examinations. Today that benefit is **not demonstrated**.

The project has two valid deliverables, which must not be conflated:

1. **Engineering:** a functional local research product, human annotations, source-identity checks, exact-K selection comparisons, omitted-span inspection, verifiable exports and a generated nonclinical demonstration.
2. **Science:** a documented sequence of real-image training, exploratory transfers, examination-held-out comparisons, negative evidence and one completed full-original-source locked Galar 11–20 evaluation. This is a reproducible research *method and record*, not a clinically effective classifier.

## Which AI experiments answer which question?

Different results are **not** one composite accuracy number. Examine the original task, case split, selection and label denominator.

1. **Anatomical region classifier** (Nov 1): a real trained compact CNN classified mouth/esophagus/stomach/small intestine/colon on sparse sampled Galar images. Examination-separated historical development accuracy **68.36%**, below always-predict-small-intestine **70.31%** (balanced accuracy 53.12% versus 20.00%). It proved integration, **not** a strong anatomy benchmark or lesion detection. [Evidence](REAL_DATA.md).
2. **Original binary blood-image CNN** (Nov 1): fitted on 4,096 sparse positions of exams 1–8, 239 positive images with **208/239 from exam 7**; checkpoint selected on already-known exams 9–10. Pooled historical-development ROC-AUC **0.7262** (exam 10 ROC-AUC **0.7079**); selected 47/213 annotated-positive images in exam 10 at 51/512 images, compared with uniform's 21. This is an **exploratory image-ranking signal**, not independent full-examination event detection. [Evidence](BLOOD_IMAGE_PILOT.md).
3. **Previously examined fitting-pool transfer** (Nov 2): the unchanged original CNN varied dramatically across examinations: AUC **0.203 on exam 23 (4 positives), 0.305 on exam 25 (14 positives), approximately 0.970 on exam 27 (26 positives), and 0.891 on exam 30 (120 positives)** in separately reported fixed-transfer runs. Tiny positive counts produce very uncertain AUCs; acquisition/case choice and exact model/score context differ between experiments. Do not pool those as a natural-prevalence clinical accuracy estimate. [Transfer 21/23/30](GALAR_FROZEN_TRANSFER_RESULTS.md), [25](GALAR25_TRANSFER_RESULTS.md), [27](GALAR27_TRANSFER_RESULTS.md).
4. **Examination-held-out fitting recipe tests** (Nov 2–3): matched three-fold training-only evaluation of a compact blood CNN reported macro AUC **0.6865** among six examinations with at least ten positive references, versus **0.463** for the tested balanced-sampling-plus-photometric-jitter recipe. Adding two verified blood-negative-labeled fitting examinations improved that macro AUC **0.6865 → 0.7078**, a modest *exploratory* change while difficult exams continued to fail. A frozen ImageNet MobileNetV3 feature probe yielded **0.6154** on the same six-exam support, and the separate fine-tuned MobileNet attempt did not consistently outperform the compact baseline. None earned clinical promotion. [CV](GALAR_EXAM_CV_RESULTS.md), [negative expansion](GALAR_NEGATIVE_EXPANSION_CV_RESULTS.md), [frozen MobileNet](GALAR_FROZEN_MOBILENET_CV_RESULTS.md), [adaptation](DOMAIN_ADAPTATION_RESULTS.md).
5. **Completed source-complete locked Galar 11–20 evaluation** (Nov 4): all **163,161** eligible original images from ten examinations were acquired/verified privately; original checkpoint and four exact-K policies were sealed before blood-outcome release. Six examinations contained positive annotation runs, four had zero and thus *undefined* recall. The **prespecified 10% primary** mean paired (score-bin minus uniform) run-recall difference is **−1.2668765 percentage points**: **49.35% vs 50.62%**, two wins, two losses and two ties. **No primary benefit demonstrated.** At **25%**, the **prespecified secondary** difference was **+9.5106442 percentage points**, substantially influenced by exam 15; it must not replace the primary. [Scientific results](GALAR_UNSEEN_11_20_RESULTS.md), [frozen design](GALAR_UNSEEN_11_20_EVALUATION_LOCK.json), [public aggregate](../experiments/galar-unseen-11-20-20261104/aggregate.json).

### What the locked experiment teaches

At 10% workload, **highest-score** selection found **305** blood-positive annotated images but reached only **28 of 154** distinct positive annotation runs; uniform selected **170** positive images yet reached **63 of 154** runs. Image enrichment can cluster on long positive stretches instead of retrieving diverse intervals. Highest-score also left a worst-case **9,129**-position omission span, versus **10** for uniform. A source-position gap is *not elapsed time*.

For the designated primary comparison, **score-bin** reached **64** runs across all ten examinations against uniform's **63**; that **pooled** outcome must not replace the equally weighted six-examination mean, which is **negative**. Examination 20 has **116 of all 154** positive runs and therefore heavily influences pooled results. The 25% descriptive difference is fragile across cases: it includes a large improvement in exam 15 and failures in exams 17 and 20. More selected images do not mechanically imply better ranking; scores and exact-K selections are recomputed at every workload.

**Do not** infer that an annotation run is a unique clinically adjudicated lesion, that zero `blood=1` means a healthy study, or that 10% of images implies a 90% reduction in physician reading time. The dataset's cases were pathology-enriched; independent patients, normal-population prevalence, prospective workflow savings, and clinical diagnostic sensitivity have not been established. The source run uses Galar's +5 annotated-frame stride and cannot be silently imposed on another dataset.

### Scientific credibility

The frozen model/queues, timestamped pre-outcome commitment, original design and after-outcome independent numerical replay make the negative result substantially more credible than favorable development-set performance. Amendment A1 concealed **binary polarity** in an eligibility audit but handled raw source CSV bytes in a controlled worker: do not portray it as wholly unprocessed or as independent-human blinding. The ten-examination evaluation cannot be rebranded as a fresh holdout after this result.

The earlier Work scientific handoff reports that the private pre-outcome prediction package/checkpoint/source index were recovered and verified by SHA and that all 200 policy selections reconcile. That report also states the **complete original-image archive was not durably retained** and **the original post-outcome execution receipts were not recovered**. These are explicit retention limitations. Public source review alone does not establish the present private cloud asset locations or lifetime.

## Competitive context and novelty discipline

- [Le Floch et al., Galar, Scientific Data 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-025-05112-7): 80 annotated examinations, ~3.5 million frames, 29 labels, multisystem multi-center. The dataset was selected from pathological studies; the source authors' reserved 61–80 are a distinct original test group. Our ten-examination result is a *different*, narrower fixed-frame-selection question, not a replacement for the dataset's benchmark.
- [CapsuleAudit, MICCAI 2026](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2026/0188-Paper0867.html): endoscopy-pretrained CNN plus temporal transformer, ~90% anatomy macro F1 and ~67% binary anomaly macro recall on a different task/evaluation. This shows temporal models are established prior art, not a directly comparable score.
- [SEE-AI, DEN Open May 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13177839/): retrospective six-hospital crossover of 249 examinations reports improved lesion sensitivity and clinician reading time with AI-annotated video. Unlike our selection-only study, it included reader evaluation and adjudicated lesions. A local frame queue must not be positioned as equivalent.
- [2026 WCE AI systematic review/meta-analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13162847/): impressive curated-study metrics coexist with limited external and prospective validation. This underscores the independent-cohort requirement, not a claim of unique novelty for Capsule Review.

**Defensible scientific niche:** *transparent, falsifiable evidence-interval retention under exactly matched image workload, with source-identity assurance, negative-result retention and inspectable omission spans*. This is a meaningful evaluation-method problem. **Novelty and patient impact remain hypotheses**, requiring an actual systematic prior-art review, relevant baselines and external/cohort collaboration.

## Recommended next milestones

**Scientific (highest value):** a **preregistered external-cohort replication of the unchanged model and four policies**, with verified patient independence where possible, exhaustive outcome annotation, a clinically reviewed ontology, native run continuity, 10% primary and 25% secondary endpoints. **Do not execute** until the nine admission gates in [external cohort qualification](EXTERNAL_COHORT_QUALIFICATION_20261104.md) are resolved and the [conditional draft](EXTERNAL_REPLICATION_DESIGN_DRAFT_20261104.md) is legitimately frozen. If no eligible data are accessible, report a no-go rather than pretending sparse labeled-image accuracy addresses the original endpoint.

**Product (parallel, no clinical data):** make the existing synthetic viewer and public results explorer independently demonstrable; use a reproducible short walkthrough covering importing, four queues, inspecting omissions, human review, verification and export. Preserve original reviewer notes and never auto-label clinical findings. This improves collaborator evaluation but **does not** fix the model's independent performance.

**Public description:** “Capsule Review is a local, audit-focused capsule-endoscopy research viewer and experimental evidence-selection framework. The CNN was trained and tested on real capsule images, but a completed locked 10% evaluation did not demonstrate better positive-annotation-run coverage than uniform sampling. Clinical utility and external validity remain unproven.”

## Audit methods and omissions

This audit inspected the public `main` README, AGENTS, tests/workflows, implementation files and dated aggregate experiment records; checked the latest merged release trajectory through PRs #33–37; and cross-checked the field's Galar, CapsuleAudit and SEE-AI sources. It did **not** access the private checkpoint, clinical source images, previous Work cloud filesystem, patient identity mappings, private post-outcome logs, or instrument human users. CI records verify software test outcomes but are not an independent security assessment. No new model was fitted, no clinical inference was run, and no protected outcome cohort was opened.
