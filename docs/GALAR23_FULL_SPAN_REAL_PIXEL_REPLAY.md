# Galar23 original-PNG full-span replay: source-verified image retention

**2026-11-03 | Previously inspected fitting examination, NOT independent biomedical validation.**

## What was run

This is the first **private original-image** check of the opt-in [full-span evidence preservation feature](FULL_SPAN_EVIDENCE_EXPORT.md) after its synthetic end-to-end CI release. We recovered the existing verified private source package (1,001 original Galar23 PNGs, including the exact 512 predeclared reference images), the actual previous 79-frame reviewer-export ZIP, its complete 512-position private imported-image manifest and its immutable scripted selection plan.

All **512 original reference PNG byte SHA-256s** were checked against the independently recorded source receipt. The original Capsule Review RGB/EXIF-transpose/PNG normalization was re-executed for all **295** images included by the full-span policy, and every resulting display SHA-256 matched the prior imported-image manifest. The earlier **79 exported display PNGs matched byte-for-byte**. A new **61,526,727-byte private full-span ZIP** was assembled and independently re-opened. All **295 exported normalized PNG hashes**, complete source-importer identity, saved-review event revisions, active interval creation events, image retention reasons and exact exported membership reconciled. ZIP CRC traversal passed.

**Reproduction scope:** The private procedure reconstructs the full-span archive **format and selection algorithm** using original pixels and archived previously generated application export records. It is **not** a byte-identical execution of the newly merged FastAPI full-span export endpoint against the original-image workspace. Separately, public GitHub CI runs the *production* endpoint and its independent verifier with actual synthetic image bytes, including the same 512-position plan. The private research ZIP, selected image IDs, reviewer notes and raw source checksums are retained only in the private Capsule Review Library.

## Real original-image retention observed

| Previously fixed scripted selection | Normal review: boundaries | Opt-in full span |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Imported original reference images | 512 | 512 |
| Exported imported-image PNGs | **79** | **295** |
| Imported marked-interval interiors retained | 24 | **240** |
| Additional retained imported images vs normal export | — | **216** |
| Explicitly blood-positive **reference images** retained, of four | **0** | **2** |

The standard boundary-only export selected 51 scripted bookmarks plus distinct marked-interval endpoints (79 unique positions). The full-span policy preserved all **272 imported positions in the 16 active marked spans** plus saved images outside the spans, leading to 295 unique images. Of the 240 strict-interior positions, 216 were **not** in the standard export. The four annotated positive reference images were already known from the prior private verification; two lie inside marked spans but outside the boundary-only export.

The **295-image export has substantially more retained images than the 79-image export**. Therefore this experiment demonstrates evidence **preservation by exporting more images**, not superior *selection accuracy at matched reader workload*. The additional imported images were not automatically viewed by a person, and image-level `blood=1` annotations are **not distinct lesions or independent clinical cases**. No blood-detection model was evaluated or promoted by this replay. The source-index positions may jump; the 512 imported images are **not the full original examination**.

## What remains unverified

The exact publisher-corrected original Galar label ZIP, **17,931,138 bytes** and SHA-256 **2f5ba84c47d343d51952e78bf22183b9d04ba8c105f625af3cf9ffb20412e52f**, could not be retrieved and authenticated in this private session. Consequently the **full original positive annotation-run denominator, directly retained original source runs, and source-run recall remain unavailable**. Our 2/4 image-level observation must not be promoted to a 50% pathology/lesion sensitivity result, nor can an interval's numerical overlap be counted as clinician detection.

Reserved Galar 11–20 and the original authors' 61–80 were not inspected, and no independent patient-level holdout was evaluated.

## Research interpretation

This artifact supports one narrow engineering statement: **when a researcher explicitly marks an interval, the new optional archive can preserve every image actually imported inside that interval, while the older concise review retains only the endpoints and separately saved frames.** The new procedure has now been checked against a prior real-image scripted review and exact original-image hashes. Whether machine learning can identify important intervals **at a useful, equal reviewer workload** remains an open and separately testable scientific question.
